Happy birthday, baby ❤️

I know you don't really like celebrating your birthday, so I don't want today to be about making you celebrate or making a huge deal out of it. I just wish I could be there with you.

If distance wasn't a thing, I'd honestly just want to spend the day with you. We'd probably be cuddling while it's raining outside, watching movies, and I'd tell you how much I love you. Because for me, it's never really been about celebrating your birthday. It's about being with you and reminding you how much you mean to me.

I love your smile, your face, and all those little expressions you make on our calls. I especially love when you get shy about coming on VC because you think you don't look good. I wish you could see yourself the way I see you. You never have to look perfect for me, baby. I just love seeing you.

We've known each other for a while, even though we lost touch for some time, and I'm so glad we found our way back to each other. I never would've imagined that reconnecting with you would lead to you becoming the best part of my life in such a beautiful way.

There have been little moments between us that I'll always remember. I still remember feeling genuinely bad when I made you cry, because the last thing I ever want is to be the reason you're hurting. I just wanted to stay with you and make sure you were okay. Looking back, it's funny how a moment like that became one of those little moments that brought us closer and made what we have feel even more special. ❤️

And of course, “rupees to bucks” and the hoola hoop 😭 — things that probably make absolutely no sense to anyone else, but somehow became ours.

Also, I haven't forgotten about the bet you lost. 😌 You still owe me a LOT of kisses the next time we meet, and unfortunately for you, I'm very good at remembering things like that. 😭❤️ So yeah… I'll be collecting. 💋

I really wish I could give you these flowers myself and just hold you close today. Until I can, I hope they remind you that someone a little too far away is thinking about you and wishing he was beside you.

Happy 21st birthday, Pragya. ❤️
